# Leonida Trophy Guide

An offline guide and tracker for all 55 Grand Theft Auto VI trophies on PlayStation 5. It lists every trophy with its official name, description and grade, a checklist saved in the browser with export and import, filters by grade and kind, a spoiler setting that hides story trophies until they are earned, a roadmap to the platinum, and a guide to each trophy with its sources.

Made for the make.fun bounty [Build the best GTA 6 trophy guide](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-trophy-guide-01).

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — tick trophies off as you earn them, filter by grade or kind, turn on the spoiler setting to hide story trophies, and follow the roadmap from the story chapters through activities and collectibles to 100% completion and the platinum.

Original guide text only: no Rockstar Games logo or official artwork is used.
